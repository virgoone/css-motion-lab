02 / Anatomy

Three nested hover zones

  1. .document-folder:hover separates the two front panels to reveal the pocket.
  2. :has(.postage:hover) raises the collection and recloses the panels behind it.
  3. Hovering the exposed rear stamp translates and rotates only that document.
